About us

Supporting NHS organisations with clinical capacity

Integrated Pain Services Limited is a UK healthcare provider delivering clinical insourcing services to NHS organisations. Although our name reflects our origins in pain services, our capability is not limited to pain management.

We work with NHS partners to provide additional clinical capacity across a range of specialties, helping organisations respond to demand, improve access to care and support elective recovery while maintaining safe, effective and patient-centred standards.

Our services are delivered by appropriately qualified and experienced healthcare professionals working within agreed NHS pathways, local policies, clinical systems and governance arrangements. We place strong emphasis on workforce assurance, professional registration, safeguarding, information governance, quality monitoring and continuous improvement.

Our insourcing model is designed to integrate with the host organisation, supporting efficient mobilisation and collaborative delivery while minimising disruption to existing clinical services.
